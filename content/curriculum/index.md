This folder houses structured learning paths for mastering complex technical domains. Each curriculum represents a commitment to deep, systematic learning - organized roadmaps from foundational concepts to production-level expertise.

These curriculums serve as guides for developing professional competence in areas that matter to me. These curriculums emerged from long conversations with AI assistants (primarily Claude and ChatGPT).

**Let's be honest**: I created these curriculums partly to shame myself into actually following through. Making learning plans public creates a forcing function. When it's just in your head or a private notebook, it's easy to abandon. When it's public, there's at least some psychological pressure to execute.

> [!IMPORTANT] This is learning in public - messy, imperfect, but accountable.
> These curriculums are living documents, they evolve as I learn more and as the field advances. The goal isn't perfection, it's progress.
