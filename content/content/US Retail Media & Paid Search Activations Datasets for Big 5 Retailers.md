## What

We have launched a new set of harmonized datasets to support **activations analysis** for the Big 5 US retailers: Amazon, Chewy, Kroger, Target, and Walmart.

These datasets enable standardized **paid search and paid media analytics** at campaign granularity and are designed to directly power downstream analytical solutions such as **Retail Business Solutions (RBS)** and **Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM)**.

### Why

Multiple analytics teams required a **single, trusted, retailer-agnostic data foundation** to answer questions around paid media and paid search effectiveness across the US retail landscape.

Key drivers:
- RBS teams required campaign-level and tactic-level performance views
- MMM teams required consistent, harmonized inputs across retailers
- Existing retailer-native datasets were fragmented and inconsistent
- No unified model existed across paid search and paid media channels

This initiative closes that gap by providing a **harmonized activations data model** aligned across retailers, channels, and tactics.

## Feature

### 1 Campaign and Structure Analytics
- Campaign-level, ad group-level, keyword-level, and product-level performance
- Strategy-driven analysis using `strategy_desc`
- Placement and targeting level attribution

### 2 Media Performance Metrics
- Net spend and reporting spend
- Impressions, clicks, views, reach
- CPC, CPM, CTR
- Video metrics including plays, completions, and completion rate
- Conversion rate and order count

### 3 Sales and Conversion Analytics
- Purchases and unit sales
- Direct attributed sales and attributed sales windows
- New-to-brand sales and halo sales
- Product-level purchase attribution

### 4 Attribution and Halo Measurement
- Attribution window tracking
- Halo ROAS and halo sales contribution
- Separation of direct vs halo impact
- New-to-brand direct vs halo splits

### 5 Product and Brand Intelligence
- Advertised product or purchased product analysis
- Product format and lifestyle tagging
- Animal and category-level rollups

### 6 Creative and Media Diagnostics
- Creative format classification
- Addressable vs non-addressable formats
- Biddable vs non-biddable formats
- Language and creative metadata
- Media vendor and sub-media tracking
