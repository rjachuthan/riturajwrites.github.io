This is where my rough book notes live when they're still raw and unprocessed. You know that feeling when you're reading something important, taking notes furiously, highlighting passages, and thinking "I'll come back and organize this properly later"? These notes are my notes on such books that I try to read.

The theory is simple: I read books, I take notes here in real-time, and then when I actually have the mental bandwidth (spoiler: I don't), I'll process these notes into proper #🌳evergreen notes that live elsewhere in the vault. Those evergreen notes will be atomic, well-connected, and actually useful for future reference. Whereas, these notes are just trying to survive.

That's why everything here is tagged as #🌱seedling - they're not finished thoughts.
